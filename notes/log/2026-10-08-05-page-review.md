# 2026-10-08: a read of the page, and two gaps

A read-through of the page and its flow, looking for gaps in the shielding and for what the page fails
to explain. Two of the findings were bugs; they are fixed here. The rest is a list of what to change in
the words and pictures, below.

## A second device adopts the seat in silence

`settle` in `site/src/main.mjs`: a browser with the synced passkey and no stored home takes the latest
seat from `SeatFactory.seatsOf` and marks it `found`. Nothing showed that flag. The console in that
browser has an empty ledger, so if the first device signed with key `n` and its transaction is still
pending, or was dropped, the second device would read `n` unchanged from the chain and sign another
approval with key `n`: two public signatures by one key, the danger case, by accident.

The fix is a warning the page can't check for itself: on a seat the browser found on chain, until this
console has signed for it once, the press screen says that another device's console holds the record,
and the hold button waits for a tick that nothing signed with key `n` is waiting there. One device per
seat is the rule; this makes the exception loud.

## The receipt says nothing about the approval

`press` took the transaction's receipt as the verdict and the Safe's nonce as whether it ran. Two cases
read wrong:

- **A front-run.** Anyone can copy the `approve` call out of the mempool and send it alone, first. It can
  only do what was signed. The approval lands in their transaction; the page's own reverts, since its
  `approve` part may not fail. The done screen would have said "Key n: signed, sent, burned" and "has
  it the ETH?", with a link to a reverted transaction.
- **A wrapped revert.** A smart-account wallet's wrapper may not revert when the call inside it does.
  Then a transaction that landed nothing reads as a success.

Now the page asks the seat where approval `n` landed (`approvedIn(n)`, then the event and the
transaction). Landed in another transaction: it says so, names theirs, and the console's ledger gets
that hash. Not landed, whatever the receipt said: the approval didn't go, and the console keeps it to
send again. For that, the console's `sent` request now takes a newer transaction hash for an approval
that is sent again; before, it kept the first one. The console fingerprint changed with it.

## What else the read found

Not bugs; what to change next, in the order it was noticed:

- The screen headed "Press the button" has no button near its heading, and the button says hold. One
  approval is three actions (hold, tap, confirm) and the page calls the whole thing a press in five
  places. Name the step "Approve", say which key is loaded, and show the three actions before the hold.
- "Seat" appears on the build screen before anything defines it.
- The steps bar is about setup and freezes on its last step. The loop that repeats has no picture; the
  sequence in [one press](../concepts/one-press.md) could be drawn in How it works.
- The ladder changes in silence after a press. The chains canvas doesn't say that the column heights
  are the hex digits of `m`, nor which three columns are the checksum.
- "What an attacker sees" belongs on the done screen, as before and after.
- The verify code and blockie have nothing to be compared with until a board exists.
- The hero's "nothing yet to attack" overstates: the curve public key is on chain from the build.
- Fund it could ride in the build transaction.
