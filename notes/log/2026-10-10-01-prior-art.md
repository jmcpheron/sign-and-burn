# 2026-10-10: a first look at prior art, and the review plan

## The ask

Find other projects that burn or rotate keys in a multisig or smart account, compare the nearest
one, and update the plan for the baseline review with what was learned.

## Found

- **wedgie-pq** (`clawdbotatg/wedgie-pq`, read at `a930b55`, five commits on 2026-10-08) is the
  nearest. WOTS with w = 16 on Poseidon2, `n` in every hash, each signature naming the next key's
  public key, a seed and a counter in a Trust M on a Pico. A STARK-checked Safe owner is planned,
  not built. Its counter is spent before signing and the signature isn't kept, so a signature that
  never lands leaves the chain expecting a key the chip has passed. Keys carry `n` alone, not a
  chain or an account.
- **Riva Labs' Ephemeral Keys** (ERC-4337) rotates during validation, so the rotation stands when
  execution reverts: the same choice as a press here. It moved off WOTS to FORS+C because dropped
  UserOps, replacements and reorgs made reuse routine.
- WOTS-39, the Solana Winternitz vaults, Qanary and SHRINCS are in
  [research.md, question 7](../research.md#7-prior-art-and-review).
- Not found: a rotating one-time key behind one owner of an ordinary Safe, or one paired with a
  passkey's curve signature.
