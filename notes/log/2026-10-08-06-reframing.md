# 2026-10-08, afternoon: reframing, open questions, and a review process

## The ask

Present the project more carefully. The cryptography isn't new. What may be new is the integration:
- a stable seat in an ordinary Safe whose key changes after every approval;
- two signatures over the same approval;
- a console that signs only what it worked out itself;
- one-time signing that survives ordinary failure.

The ask came with seven areas to investigate before stronger claims. It also asked for an outside
review built into any contract work.

## Found while writing it: a threat row that said too much

The page, `notes/concepts/trust.md` and KICKOFF.md all had this row:

> A bug in the one-time code: **partly**. The curve signature still guards, unless curves are broken
> too.

That is only half right.
- The passkey signs `c`, which binds the chain, the seat, the Safe, `n` and the Safe transaction.
- It doesn't sign the next key. The seeds arrive in the same tap, after the challenge was fixed. That
  is why there are two messages (KICKOFF.md, "One tap").
- So suppose someone could forge one-time signatures and saw an approval waiting to land. They could
  keep its valid curve signature, name their own next key, and take the seat.
- The curve signature still stops them approving another transaction.

The row now says so, on the page and in the notes. It is open question 1 in research.md, with a
candidate v2 that works out the next key one press early, so the passkey can sign it. Nothing in the
contracts changed.

Two more rows were added, both "no":
- **A lost ledger, or a synced passkey on a second device.** This was already in the guardrail's
  notes, but not in the table.
- **Other owners who can reach the threshold without the seat.**
