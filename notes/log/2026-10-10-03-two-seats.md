# 2026-10-10: two seats, and 2 of 3

## The ask

After the wallet page: a backup that doesn't give up the protection. Two rotating seats, and a 2 of 3
with two seats and a regular wallet, so the wallet can start a transaction and a seat confirms it.

Two things were cleared up first. A 2 of 2 with two seats protects, but it has no backup: lose either
passkey and the Safe is stuck. The backup is 1 of 2 (either seat alone) or 2 of 3 with the wallet. In
2 of 3 every set of owners that reaches 2 holds a seat, so a broken curve that forges the wallet's
vote still needs a one-time key. Neither needs a change to the contracts: `Seat.approve` takes the
Safe as an argument, and the Safe's address is inside what both signatures cover. Nor to the
console: it builds `c` and `m` from whatever Safe the request names, and its ledger is per seat.

## What changed

- **Other Safes.** The wallet page keeps the Safes this seat is in (`sab.safes`), opens any of them,
  and shows the seat's address to copy into another Safe's "Add an owner".
- **A proposal link**, `sign-and-burn/proposal/v1` (`src/pay.mjs`): a transaction waiting for votes,
  from one owner's device to another's. It holds the Safe and the transaction's fields: nothing
  signed, no hash. The other device's console works out the hash and shows the same check code, and
  the votes so far are read from the chain. It opens only on a device whose seat or wallet is an
  owner of that Safe, and only for a nonce the Safe hasn't run.
- **Seats are recognised.** An owner counts as a seat when it names a passkey signer and the
  SeatFactory lists it among that signer's seats (`chain.isSeat`). Before, every other seat showed
  as "A contract", and the verdict called it an ordinary owner.
- **The verdict is two lines now.** Whether ordinary keys can reach the threshold with no seat (then a
  broken curve takes the Safe), and whether the other owners can reach it without this browser's
  seat (a backup if its passkey is lost).
- **Key n is bound to its Safe.** If the seat signed an approval for one Safe and it hasn't landed,
  the wallet page won't let it approve for another, and offers to open that one. The main page shows
  that approval reviewed against its own Safe, offers it as a link, and points to the wallet page.
  Before, it would have worked out the hash against the home Safe.

The console is unchanged, so its fingerprint is too. The contracts are unchanged.

## What was checked, and how

- `npm test`: a proposal link round-trips the Safe and the fields, holds no hash and nothing signed,
  isn't mistaken for a payment link either way, and seven bad links are refused.
- `npm run e2e`: all 115 checks pass. The new ones are item 17 in [testing/page.md](../testing/page.md):
  a second browser with its own passkey and Safe (its build paid by the wallet in that browser, which
  the e2e hadn't driven before), 1 of 2 with two seats, 2 of 3 with the wallet, and a send the wallet
  starts and the second seat finishes by link.
- `npm run build`, twice: the same bytes.

Not checked: on Base Sepolia by hand; two real devices; two seats whose passkeys sync to one account
(one takeover would reach both, and the verdict can't see that).

## Found along the way

- The e2e's check of the second browser's heading failed once: the eyebrow is uppercase in CSS, so the
  page's text says "A SAFE YOUR SEAT IS IN". The check ignores case now, as the first heading's did.
- In the e2e both browsers share one test wallet. On real devices the second would usually have its own.
