# 2026-10-10: a lagging RPC, and a false "didn't land"

## Found live

On Base Sepolia, the first seat (`0xCE5fa5…`) voted on the second Safe (`0x1EF7Ef…`), signed on one
device and sent by link from another device's wallet. The paying page said: "The transaction went
through (0x35d0c397…3f80b8), but approval 4 didn't land: the seat is still at key 4."

The chain says otherwise, read with `cast` the same day: `approvedIn(4)` is block 47,920,999, the
seat is at key 5, and the transaction's logs hold the seat's `Approved`, the Safe's `ApproveHash` and
`ExecutionSuccess`. Everything worked; the check didn't.

## Why

`sepolia.base.org` spreads requests over several nodes. The wallet's receipt came from a node that had
the block; the page's next read (`approvedIn(4)`, at "latest") reached one that didn't yet. A single
Anvil, as the e2e used, never lags, so it never showed. The same lag could also make a page say the
Safe hadn't run a transaction it had.

## What changed

- `chain.readAt`: a read as of a given block (a receipt's). A node that doesn't have that block yet
  refuses the read, and it waits and asks again: every 250 ms locally, every second on Base Sepolia,
  up to 20 times.
- After a press, after a payment by link, and after a seat's vote on the wallet page, whether the
  approval landed and whether the Safe ran it are read as of the receipt's block (`approvalOnChain`
  with `at`, `nonceAt`).
- The e2e's chain now sits behind a proxy on the page's RPC port. With `lag.on` it serves state as of
  a block that appeared at least 2.5 s ago, and refuses reads at newer blocks ("header not found"),
  while receipts and block numbers come from the newest block. Two checks run with it on: a press on
  the main page, and a signed approval sent by link from another browser, the case found live.

## What was checked, and how

- Without the fix, the lagging e2e failed as the live page did: the seat at key 2 and the Safe's nonce
  moved on chain, and the page offered "Hold to send approval 1 again". With it, all 125 checks pass.
- Not checked: the fix on Base Sepolia itself. Other reads right after a transaction (the side panels,
  a build's "is it built yet") can still show the old state for a moment; the pages read again on
  their next refresh.
