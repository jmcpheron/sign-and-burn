# 2026-10-10: sending tokens, and swaps on Base Sepolia

## The ask

Send tokens from the Safe, and some pointers on swapping on Base Sepolia.

## What changed

- **Send picks an asset** on the wallet page: ETH, the tokens the console knows (USDC on Base Sepolia,
  pinned in `console/cfg.py` since the start), and tokens added by address (`sab.tokens`). A token
  send is a Safe transaction to the token's `transfer`; the owners vote on it like any other.
- **Balances**: the header shows the Safe's token balances beside its ETH, and Send says how much of
  the chosen asset the Safe holds. The hold waits when the Safe has less than it sends.
- **A token the console doesn't know** works too. The page reads its symbol and decimals from its
  contract, for its own display only. The console reviews it as "SEND TOKEN" in raw units (amber, not
  red), and the page says so before the review.
- **`tools/chain/test-token.mjs`**: the least ERC-20 a send needs (balanceOf, transfer with its event,
  decimals, symbol), assembled to bytecode there, so the e2e needs no compiler. The e2e puts it at Base
  Sepolia's USDC address on the local chain, and at one the console doesn't know. It is not one of
  `contracts/`, and is never deployed on a real chain.
- The console's review text now wraps anywhere: the note for a token it doesn't know holds a long
  address, which pushed the wallet page sideways at phone width (the e2e's width check found it).

The console and the contracts are unchanged.

## What was checked, and how

- The test token against a scratch Anvil with `cast`: symbol, decimals, a balance, a transfer and its
  log, `true` returned, and a revert with too little.
- `npm run e2e`: all 139 checks pass (item 16 in [testing/page.md](../testing/page.md)).

## Swaps on Base Sepolia: what is there

Read from Base Sepolia with `cast` on 2026-10-10:
- Uniswap v3 is deployed: the v3 factory `0x4752ba5DBc23f44D87826276BF6Fd6b1C372aD24`, SwapRouter02
  `0x94cC0AaC535CCDB3C01d6787D6413C739ae12bc4`, and the Universal Router
  `0x050E797f3625EC8785265e1d9BDd4799b97528A1` all have code.
- WETH (`0x4200…0006`) / USDC pools exist at 0.05%, 0.3% and 1%. The 0.3% pool held about 33,900 USDC
  and 5.2 WETH; testnet prices are whatever its liquidity says.
- Circle's faucet (faucet.circle.com) sends test USDC on Base Sepolia, no account needed.

The console can't approve a swap from the Safe today: a router call is a call it can't read, so it
refuses. That would take pinning WETH and the router in `cfg.py` and decoding the router's calls in
`decode.py`: a console change, with a new fingerprint. Not done.
